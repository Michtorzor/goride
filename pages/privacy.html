Privacy Policy

GoRide respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, application, ride services and delivery services.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the services you use, GoRide may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Pickup and destination information
  • Delivery information
  • Account information
  • Payment-related information
  • Information you provide when contacting support

2. Location Information

When you use ride or delivery services, GoRide may process location information necessary to provide the requested service, such as pickup and destination locations.

3. How We Use Your Information

GoRide may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process ride and delivery requests.
  • Calculate fares and delivery charges.
  • Communicate with you about your requests.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve our services and website.
  • Detect and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Sharing Information

GoRide may share relevant information with riders, delivery partners, service providers and other parties where necessary to provide requested services.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, GoRide or others.

5. Payment Information

Payment transactions may be processed by third-party payment providers. GoRide may not directly store all payment information, depending on the payment method and provider used.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

GoRide may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, understand how users interact with our website and improve website functionality.

7. Data Security

GoRide takes reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

GoRide may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction or deletion of certain information.

You may contact GoRide to make a privacy-related request.

10. Children's Privacy

GoRide services are not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use the relevant services.

11. Third-Party Services

GoRide may use third-party services such as payment providers, mapping services, analytics tools and communication services. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

GoRide may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page together with a revised update date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to make a privacy-related request, please contact GoRide through the contact information provided on our website.
