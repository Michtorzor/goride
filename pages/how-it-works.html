HOW GORIDE WORKS

One Platform. Three Ways to Get Moving.

Whether you are sending a package, delivering packages as a rider, or managing a fleet, GoRide gives you a clear process to get started.

FOR CUSTOMERS

Send a Package

From registration to delivery, the customer process is designed to keep your package moving.

01

Create Your Account

Register your customer account with your basic information.

02

Enter Locations

Enter the pickup and drop-off locations for your package.

03

Get Your Fare

Enter the required package information and view the applicable delivery fare.

04

Confirm Booking

Confirm your delivery request and provide the package information required.

05

Rider Assigned

A rider can be assigned to handle the delivery.

06

Track Delivery

Follow the delivery progress and rider status.

07

Receive Package

Your package reaches the specified destination.

Send a Package
FOR PROFESSIONAL RIDERS

Become a GoRide Rider

Professional riders can register and participate in GoRide delivery operations.

01

Register as a Rider

Choose the professional rider option and complete the registration form.

02

Submit Your Details

Provide your personal information, experience, bike details and required licence information.

03

Verification

Your submitted information can be reviewed before approval.

04

Get Approved

Once approved, you can participate in available delivery operations.

05

Receive Requests

View available delivery opportunities assigned or offered to you.

06

Complete Deliveries

Pick up packages and deliver them to their destinations.

07

Track Earnings

Review completed delivery and earnings information.

Register as a Rider
FOR FLEET OWNERS

Manage Your Delivery Fleet

Fleet owners can manage bikes, riders and delivery operations from the GoRide fleet system.

01

Register Your Fleet

Create a GoRide fleet owner account and provide your business or fleet information.

02

Add Your Bikes

Add the motorcycles or bikes that form part of your fleet.

03

Add Your Riders

Register or add professional riders working with your fleet.

04

Assign Riders

Connect riders with available bikes and organize your fleet.

05

Monitor Deliveries

Keep track of fleet delivery activities and rider operations.

06

Manage Operations

Manage your bikes, riders, assignments and delivery records.

07

Review Performance

View available operational and earnings information for your fleet.
