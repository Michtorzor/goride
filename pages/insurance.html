Insurance

Information about delivery protection and package responsibility.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Delivery Protection

GoRide aims to provide a safe and reliable delivery experience. Any insurance or protection available for a particular delivery will depend on the applicable service, terms and coverage in effect at the time of the delivery.

2. Customer Responsibility

Customers are responsible for ensuring that packages are properly packaged and accurately described before handing them to a rider.

3. Prohibited Items

Items that are prohibited under applicable law or GoRide delivery rules may not qualify for any available protection.

4. Package Damage or Loss

If a customer believes that a package has been damaged, lost or otherwise affected during delivery, the customer should contact GoRide as soon as possible and provide relevant delivery information.

5. Package Photographs

GoRide may request a photograph of a package before delivery. A package photograph may help document the condition or identity of an item at the time of collection.

6. Coverage Limitations

Any available insurance or compensation may be subject to applicable limits, exclusions, eligibility requirements and documentation.

7. Third-Party Insurance

Where an insurance provider or other third party is involved, claims may be subject to that provider's terms, conditions and procedures.

8. Contacting GoRide

For questions about a delivery incident or available protection, please contact GoRide through the contact details provided on the website.
