Cookies are small data files that websites may store on a user's device. They can help websites remember information and provide certain functionality.

GoRide may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, improve performance and understand how visitors interact with the website.

4. Analytics

If analytics services are implemented on the website, cookies or similar technologies may be used to help understand website traffic and usage.

5. Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow users to control or delete cookies through their browser settings.

6. Third-Party Services
