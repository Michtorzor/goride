Security

Our approach to protecting accounts and delivery information.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Our Commitment

GoRide is committed to taking reasonable steps to protect information provided through our website and delivery services.

2. Account Security

Users should choose strong passwords and keep their login information confidential. Users should not share their passwords with other people.

3. Personal Information

Personal information should only be accessed by authorized persons or systems that require it for legitimate service purposes.

4. Delivery Information

Pickup locations, delivery locations, contact details and package information should be handled responsibly and used for legitimate delivery purposes.

5. Suspicious Activity

If you notice suspicious activity involving your GoRide account, contact GoRide through the contact information provided on our website as soon as possible.

6. Website Security

We aim to maintain reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect our website and information. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Keeping Your Information Safe

Avoid sharing passwords, verification codes or other sensitive account information with people who are not authorized to access your account.

8. Reporting a Security Issue

If you discover a possible security issue affecting GoRide, please contact us through the contact information provided on our website.
