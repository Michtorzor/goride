WHO WE ARE

Building a Better Way to Move

GoRide is a modern delivery and logistics platform created to make the movement of packages, goods and services easier, faster and more convenient.

Our platform brings different people within the delivery ecosystem together. Customers can request deliveries, riders can access delivery opportunities, fleet owners can manage their operations, and businesses can use delivery services to support their daily activities.

We believe that delivery should be simple, transparent and accessible. That is why GoRide is designed around the needs of everyone involved in the delivery process.

Fast

We focus on making delivery requests and transportation processes simple and efficient.

Safe

We promote responsible delivery practices and trusted participation across the platform.

Reliable

GoRide is designed to give customers, riders, fleet owners and businesses a dependable platform to work with.
